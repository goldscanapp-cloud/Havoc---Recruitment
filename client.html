FAQ
Common questions.
What exactly is a Havoc?
A Havoc is a vetted, professional individual hired for their presence — not security. They are commanding, composed, and professional. Think of them as presence on demand — someone whose size and presentation holds a room without saying a word.
How quickly can I get a Havoc?
For planned bookings we confirm within the hour. For same-day or urgent bookings contact us directly via WhatsApp and we'll do our best to accommodate.
Can I specify how they dress?
Yes — you tell us the dress code and your Havoc will arrive dressed exactly as requested. Suit, all black, smart casual — whatever the occasion requires.
Are Havocs trained in security?
HAVOC is a presence platform, not a security company. Havocs are not trained security personnel and should not be treated as such. They are professional, composed individuals hired for their presence and demeanour. For licensed security services, please use a registered security provider.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the booking receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable. No-shows are charged in full.
Do you operate outside South Africa?
Yes — we are available in multiple markets including UAE, USA, UK, Australia, Singapore, Canada, and Europe. Submit a booking request and we'll confirm availability in your city.
